# Homepage image guide

## Homepage hero banner

Use two image files only when the hero uses an image: desktop/tablet at **1920 × 1080 px** and mobile at **900 × 1600 px**. Upload them in the Hero image and Mobile hero image fields.

The homepage video uses a desktop video at **1600 × 900 px** or **1920 × 1080 px**. A mobile video at **720 × 1280 px** is optional when the framing differs.

## All other homepage photos

Use one image file for all devices. The website crops it responsively.

- About gallery: **960 × 1080 px**
- Programs cards: **720 × 1200 px**
- Life at School cards: **1000 × 940 px**
- Vidya Way cards: **840 × 910 px**
- Quick Facts: **1000 × 750 px**
- Trividha Shikshana mascots: **600 × 720 px**, transparent PNG or WebP

Keep the people and main subject near the image centre. Upload images through WordPress and add descriptive alt text. For sizes on other pages, see [IMAGE-SIZES.txt](IMAGE-SIZES.txt).

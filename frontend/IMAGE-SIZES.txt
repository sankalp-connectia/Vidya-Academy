VIDYA ACADEMY — IMAGE SIZE GUIDE

GENERAL RULE
Only hero banners use two image files. Add a desktop/tablet image and a separate mobile image in the hero fields. The mobile image is used at 768 px and below.
All other photos use one image file for desktop, tablet, and mobile. The website crops the same image responsively. Keep faces and important details near the centre.

HERO BANNERS — TWO IMAGES
Desktop/tablet: 1920 × 1080 px
Mobile: 900 × 1600 px
Use for the homepage hero image, About hero, Programs hero, Campus hero, Motion-In hero, Events hero, Achievements hero, and SEO landing page hero.

EVENT AND ACHIEVEMENT DETAIL HERO — TWO IMAGES
Desktop/tablet: 1600 × 1200 px
Mobile: 900 × 1200 px
Use the separate desktop and mobile hero image fields for each event or achievement.

BLOG ARTICLE HERO — TWO IMAGES
Desktop/tablet: 1600 × 1200 px
Mobile: 900 × 1200 px
Use the featured desktop image and optional mobile hero image. If the mobile field is blank, the desktop image is used.

HOMEPAGE PHOTOS — ONE IMAGE EACH
About gallery: 960 × 1080 px
Programs cards: 720 × 1200 px
Life at School cards: 1000 × 940 px
Vidya Way cards: 840 × 910 px
Quick Facts photos: 1000 × 750 px
Trividha Shikshana mascots: 600 × 720 px; use transparent PNG or WebP.

OTHER PHOTOS — ONE IMAGE EACH
Campus grounds and buildings: 1600 × 1000 px
About leadership portraits: 1000 × 1200 px
Story, event, achievement, blog, and SEO gallery photos: 1600 × 1000 px
Motion-In cards: 1200 × 1500 px
Blog article gallery: 1600 × 1000 px per photo

LOGOS AND MASCOTS — ONE IMAGE EACH
Navbar logo: 800 × 240 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
Trividha Shikshana logo: 1200 × 1028 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
FAQ mascot: 600 × 720 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
Admissions mascot: 900 × 1100 px, transparent PNG or WebP.

HOMEPAGE HERO VIDEO
Desktop video: 1600 × 900 px or 1920 × 1080 px.
Optional mobile video: 720 × 1280 px, only when its framing differs from desktop.
Use muted H.264 MP4. Host very large videos on a streaming service or object storage.

UPLOAD NOTES
Upload through the WordPress Media Library. Choose desktop and mobile files only for hero images. For all other photo fields, upload one file. Add useful alt text and keep the subject inside the central safe area.

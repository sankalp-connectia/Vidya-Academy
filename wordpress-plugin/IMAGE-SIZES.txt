VIDYA ACADEMY — WORDPRESS IMAGE SIZE GUIDE

IMAGE UPLOAD RULE
Only fields labeled “Desktop hero image” need a second, optional phone image.
Every other photo uses one upload for desktop, tablet, and phone. The website crops it responsively.

HERO BANNERS — TWO IMAGES
Desktop and tablet: 1920 × 1080 px
Phone: 900 × 1600 px
Use the separate Desktop hero image and Mobile hero image fields. The phone image is used at 768 px wide and below. If it is blank, the desktop image is reused.

EVENT AND ACHIEVEMENT STORY HEROES — TWO IMAGES
Desktop and tablet: 1600 × 1200 px
Phone: 900 × 1200 px
Use these sizes for the hero image inside an individual event or achievement detail page.

BLOG ARTICLE HERO — TWO IMAGES
Desktop and tablet: 1600 × 1200 px
Phone: 900 × 1200 px
The phone image is optional. If it is blank, the desktop featured image is reused.

OTHER PHOTO FIELDS — ONE IMAGE EACH
Homepage gallery: 960 × 1080 px
Program card: 720 × 1200 px
Life at School card: 1000 × 940 px
Vidya Way card: 840 × 910 px
Quick Facts photo: 1000 × 750 px
Campus, story, event, achievement, blog, or SEO gallery photo: 1600 × 1000 px
Leadership portrait: 1000 × 1200 px

LOGOS AND MASCOTS — ONE IMAGE EACH
Navbar logo: 800 × 240 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
Trividha Shikshana logo: 1200 × 1028 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
FAQ and learning-card mascot: 600 × 720 px, transparent PNG or WebP.
Admissions mascot: 900 × 1100 px, transparent PNG or WebP.

Keep faces and important details inside the central area of one-image photos. Use the phone hero field only when its framing needs to differ from desktop.
